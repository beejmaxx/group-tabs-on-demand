# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Group Tabs on Demand does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data,
browsing history, website content, or usage analytics.

The extension reads the URLs of tabs in the current browser window only after
the user clicks the toolbar button or invokes the keyboard shortcut. URLs are
used locally to calculate domain names and organize Chrome tab groups. They are
not persisted or sent anywhere.

The extension has no servers, remote code, advertising, analytics, accounts,
or third-party data sharing. Its functionality is fully contained in the
installed extension.

## Permissions

- `tabs`: required to read tab URLs and place tabs into groups.
- `tabGroups`: required to create, name, color, and update Chrome tab groups.

Questions or privacy concerns can be filed through the project's
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/beejmaxx/group-tabs-on-demand/issues).
